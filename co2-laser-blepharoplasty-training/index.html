What Is CO2 Laser Blepharoplasty Training?

ScalprumPro is Beauty Expert Summit's advanced blepharoplasty training track — a practical masterclass in CO2 laser eyelid surgery, built around the same live-surgery faculty presenting on the summit's main stage. Rather than a lecture-only course, it is a hands-on blepharoplasty training day: theory in the morning, a live video demonstration, then direct practice on biological models under expert supervision.

It's designed for surgeons who already perform eyelid or periorbital procedures and want structured, supervised experience with CO2 laser technique specifically — laser setup, tissue handling, complication prevention, and the surgical algorithms that differ from blade-based blepharoplasty.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CO2 laser blepharoplasty?
CO2 laser blepharoplasty is an eyelid surgery technique that uses a carbon dioxide laser to remove or reposition excess skin, fat and muscle around the eyes, combining precise tissue cutting with immediate coagulation for a cleaner surgical field and faster recovery than traditional blade techniques.
Who can attend the ScalprumPro CO2 laser blepharoplasty training?
The course is designed for licensed ophthalmologists, plastic surgeons and oculoplastic surgeons who want hands-on experience with CO2 laser techniques in periorbital and eyelid surgery. Prior surgical experience is expected; this is an advanced, practical masterclass rather than an introductory course.
Where can I learn CO2 laser blepharoplasty in Europe?
The ScalprumPro intensive wet lab takes place at VISSUM Clinic in Alicante, Spain, on 29 November 2026, as part of Beauty Expert Summit. It combines a plenary session, a live video demonstration and hands-on practice on biological models under the direct supervision of Dr. Serhii Havryliuk and Dr. Igor Janik.
What is included in the ScalprumPro wet lab price?

Is there live CO2 laser blepharoplasty surgery at Beauty Expert Summit?
Yes. On Day 1 of the summit, a live CO2 laser blepharoplasty / ptosis correction procedure is performed and moderated on stage, alongside lectures on modern blepharoplasty technique, before the ScalprumPro wet lab session on Day 2.
